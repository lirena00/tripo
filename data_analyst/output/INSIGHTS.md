Across 5 group trips (151 expenses, INR 517,930 total), Trip Splitter shows how unevenly trip bills pile up.
The top three categories are Stay (32.3%), Food (26.6%), and Activities (12.5%), keeping accommodation realistic without hiding daily food and activity costs.
Aarav (Goa Beach Week) paid most often with 12 bills, while Aarav (Goa Beach Week) fronted the highest total at INR 62,500.
Every trip shows the 'forgetful friend' pattern—members who paid only 0 to 2 bills (Tara (0), Neel (0), Yash (1), Ishaan (1), Om (0)) while still sharing daily costs.
Roughly 36.8% of expenses per trip are partial splits among subsets of friends, proving simple even-for-all calculators get group balances wrong.
Instead of 62 naive pairwise transfers across all 5 trips, our settle-up reduces 62 payments to 22 (saving 40 transactions).
